I’m sorry.

My Coco,

I’m sorry I haven’t given you the time and attention you deserve. I haven’t shown up for you the way I should have, and I understand that saying I care isn’t enough if my actions don’t show it.

When we’re far apart, a call or a little check-in can be how we feel close. I’m sorry I haven’t made enough time for those moments with you. I care about you, Coco, and I love you.

I want us to pick times to call that work for both of us, put distractions aside when we talk, and make room for the ordinary details of each other’s days. I want you to feel that I’m here with you, even from far away.

When you feel ready, I’d like to hear how you’ve been feeling and what you need from me. I want to follow these words with effort you can actually feel.

You don’t have to reply right now. Take the time and space you need.

I love you, Coco.
Always, me ♡
